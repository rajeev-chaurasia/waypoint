# Global Corporate Spend and Procurement Policy
Version: 1.0.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope and Objective (SEC-1)
Line 1: This policy applies to all global employees, contractors, and business units.
Line 2: All company expenditures must serve a direct business purpose, represent market value, and adhere to compliance routing.
Line 3: Splitting purchases into multiple smaller transactions to circumvent approval thresholds is strictly prohibited.
Line 4: Requests submitted without vendor details, itemized cost breakdowns, or legitimate justification will be returned for clarification.

## 2. Software, Cloud, and AI Services (SEC-2)
Line 5: All new SaaS tools, cloud infrastructure, and software licenses require explicit approval prior to purchase or trial deployment.
Line 6: Software subscriptions exceeding $1,000.00 annually require mandatory Security and IT Operations review for data protection.
Line 7: Use of commercial generative AI tools processing confidential company or customer data requires Security approval and SOC2 Type II verification.
Line 8: Software purchases under $500.00 from pre-approved internal app catalogs may proceed with direct manager approval only.
Line 9: Multi-year software commitments (>12 months) require Legal Counsel review and CFO authorization regardless of total spend.

## 3. IT Hardware and Physical Equipment (SEC-3)
Line 10: Standard employee workstation hardware must be provisioned through the official IT procurement portal.
Line 11: Peripheral devices, monitors, and home office equipment exceeding $500.00 require IT Operations department verification.
Line 12: Mobile devices and cellular service plans must be requested through Facilities with departmental VP sign-off.
Line 13: Hardware purchases exceeding $10,000.00 require formal comparison quotes from at least two verified suppliers.

## 4. Travel, Meals, and Entertainment (SEC-4)
Line 14: Business travel must be booked at least 14 days in advance using standard economy class for flights under 6 hours.
Line 15: Single meal reimbursements must not exceed $75.00 per person for dinner or $35.00 per person for lunch/breakfast.
Line 16: Team offsites, dinners, or entertainment events exceeding $1,500.00 total require prior written approval from the Department VP.
Line 17: Alcohol-only expenditures without an accompanying business meal are non-reimbursable.

## 5. Professional Services, Legal, and Contractors (SEC-5)
Line 18: Engaging third-party consultants, marketing agencies, or contractors requires an executed Statement of Work (SOW) and Master Services Agreement (MSA).
Line 19: Consulting agreements exceeding $5,000.00 require Legal Counsel review and approval prior to contractor onboarding.
Line 20: Engagements exceeding $50,000.00 must have Executive Committee or Board of Directors sign-off.

## 6. Restricted Vendors and Compliance Screening (SEC-6)
Line 21: Any vendor appearing on government sanctions lists or lacking verified tax identification (W-9 / W-8BEN) cannot be onboarded.
Line 22: High-risk offshore data processors must provide an active ISO 27001 or SOC2 compliance certification.
Line 23: Vendors owned by or employing immediate family members of the requesting employee must be disclosed as a conflict of interest.

## 7. Gifts, Hospitality, and Prohibited Items (SEC-7)
Line 24: Personal expenses, including gym memberships, domestic utility bills, and personal transit passes, are strictly non-reimbursable.
Line 25: Corporate gifts provided to external clients must not exceed $100.00 per recipient per calendar year.
Line 26: Giving or receiving cash, gift cards, or cryptocurrency as business gifts is strictly prohibited under anti-bribery regulations.
Line 27: Unsubstantiated miscellaneous expenses titled 'miscellaneous' or 'general expenses' without itemized invoices will be rejected.
